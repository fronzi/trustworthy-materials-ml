# Figure caveats

- `fig1_benchmark_shift`: error bars are standard deviations across folds. Cluster folds are not equally hard.
- `fig2_uncertainty_calibration`: marginal coverage only. Good average coverage does not imply coverage for each chemistry.
- `fig3_active_learning`: retrospective simulation on existing data, with 10 seeds and 1.96 standard-error bands.
- `fig4_quantum_sensing`: simulated device with assumed parameters. The Cramér-Rao line is a local reference.
- `fig5_kan_descriptors`: descriptor association in one fitted ensemble, not a causal ranking.
